How to Play

1. Upload an audio file from your device

2. Wait for the beatmap to be generated

3. Notes will fall from the top

4. Tap the correct button when notes reach the hit area

5. Build combos for higher scores!


Controls:

Use A, S, D, F keys or tap the buttons

Each key/button corresponds to a lane
